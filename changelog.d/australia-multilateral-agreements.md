### Added

- 21 multilateral treaties to which Australia is a party, found missing by walking the DFAT
  Australian Treaties Database and added as `treaty_body` records. Each has its parties roster
  from the depositary or treaty secretariat, checked on 2026-11-22. Signatories that have not
  ratified are left out.
  - Geneva law (`humanitarian` / `armscontrol`, roster from the ICRC IHL Treaties Database):
    `GCI` (Q19136672), `GCII` (Q583921) and `GCIII` (Q3984882), each with 196 States parties;
    Additional Protocols `API` (175, Q2229133) and `APII` (170, Q2401715); and the 1925 Geneva
    gas protocol `GENEVAPROTOCOL1925` (146, Q605626).
  - Hague Conference conventions (`legal`, roster from the HCCH status tables): `APOSTILLE`
    (130, Q2032476), `CHILDABDUCTION1980` (102, Q1566367) and `ADOPTION1993` (107, Q2622307).
    China is not listed for the Child Abduction Convention: HCCH lists it only for the
    continued application to Hong Kong and Macao.
  - IMO conventions (`maritime`, roster from the IMO Status Book Vol. II of 30 October 2026):
    `SUA1988` (168, Q14934146), `SUAFIXEDPLATFORMS1988` (157, Q15991246),
    `LONDONCONVENTION1972` (87, Q1156234) and `LONDONPROTOCOL1996` (56).
  - `PLASTICEXPLOSIVES1991` (Convention on the Marking of Plastic Explosives, 157 parties,
    Q15991245), from the ICAO list of parties.
  - UNESCO conventions, from the UNESCO Legal Affairs States Parties export: `UNESCO1970`
    (illicit trade in cultural property, 150, Q16958997), `UNESCO2005` (diversity of cultural
    expressions, 162 States and the European Union, Q253300) and `ANTIDOPING2005`
    (International Convention against Doping in Sport, 192, Q15991223).
  - `OECDANTIBRIBERY` (OECD Anti-Bribery Convention, 46 parties, Q626826), from the OECD
    ratification status of August 2024, the latest the OECD publishes.
  - `BEPSMLI` (BEPS Multilateral Instrument, 89 parties, Q39058243), from the OECD
    signatories and parties list of 12 November 2026. Guernsey, Jersey and the Isle of Man
    are parties in their own right; Curaçao and Hong Kong are covered by the Netherlands and
    China and are not listed.
  - `GPA` (WTO Agreement on Government Procurement, 22 parties, Q331553), from the WTO
    parties page. The European Union is one party and its member States are not listed
    separately.
  - `TAC` (Treaty of Amity and Cooperation in Southeast Asia, 62 High Contracting Parties
    including the European Union, Q1054948), from the ASEAN Legal Instruments database.
- `LONDONPROTOCOL1996` has a `wikidata_exclusions.yaml` entry: Wikidata has no item for the
  1996 Protocol apart from the 1972 Convention.
- The Convention on the Physical Protection of Nuclear Material, the Convention on Nuclear
  Safety, the International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture and
  the FAO Port State Measures Agreement are not added: the IAEA and FAO sites refuse automated
  reads.
